{%- comment -%} parts/sport-card.html: one sport on the landing page: a card with the sport's name, its one-line description, a window with a preview of its demo league in the sport's own look (the top of the standings and the latest result), and the link to the demo; or, for a sport that is not built yet, a card with a "Coming soon" tag and no window and no link. Params: sport (an entry of landing.json "sports": {name, blurb, url, demo_label, layout, window {league, standings, teams, latest}}) or soon (an entry of "coming_soon": {name, blurb}). Reads nothing else, and never asks which sport it is: the words (the section names, the legend, the result card's foot) arrive as data. The window is the existing standings table and result card, run on the demo's own league: this include saves `league` and `stats`, sets them to the window's league and to the window itself (which holds the `teams` and `standings` the table reads), and puts both back. Every url in the window already points into the demo. The window carries data-layout (the sport's look; its tokens live on that element), class st-page and no data-theme: without JavaScript it follows the phone's colour scheme, as the page does. assets/js/site.js copies the page's data-theme onto every .st-sport-card__window when the sun/moon button is pressed.
